# Delivery Checklist

## Target and state

- Correct profile/workspace, video, clip, project, sequence, and current artifact.
- Current timeline/layers re-read after the last mutation.
- Correct time domains and no stale downstream render/export.
- User's platform, aspect, duration, quality, copy, and definition of done captured.

## Story and edit

- Hook/promise is clear without misleading context.
- Required thought/quote is complete and transcript-faithful.
- Pacing, cuts, transitions, and ending are intentional.
- No unintended gaps, duplicate frames/audio, clipped words, or boundary jumps.

## Picture and framing

- Requested aspect/resolution and correct orientation.
- Active subjects, products, slides, demos, logos, and text remain readable.
- Crop/layout transitions are stable and motivated.
- B-Roll and Alter timing/application are correct.
- Generated media identity, anatomy, objects, continuity, and crop pass inspection.
- No flicker, morphing, warping, unwanted text/logo, or unrequested subjects.

## Captions and graphics

- Caption words/timing/speaker association match the approved source.
- Spelling, punctuation, line breaks, reading speed, safe areas, and contrast are usable.
- Exact enterprise style fields/hash match current canonical state.
- Graphics use approved text/assets and enter/exit at intended times.

## Audio

- Final mixed artifact was heard.
- Dialogue is intelligible and source audio behaves as requested.
- Voiceover script/pronunciation/timing are correct.
- Music/SFX support rather than mask speech; no unrequested vocals.
- Ducking, fades, loop seams, sync, loudness, and peaks pass.
- No clipping, clicks, pops, abrupt silence, or doubled layers.

## Technical artifact

- Required render/export status is complete, playable, and current.
- Duration, aspect, resolution, container, and audio presence match the request.
- Download/review URL or artifact is accessible without treating an expiring signed URL as durable evidence.
- Export is bound to current editor state; enterprise lineage/fingerprint/outro/duration are exact where required.

## Delivery and publishing authority

- Destination account IDs were refreshed and explicitly pinned.
- Copy/title/time and exact artifact were approved.
- Enterprise deliverable is client-selected, not only ready.
- Spend estimate/cap/approval and per-platform results are retained.

## Classification

Use:

```text
FAIL: prevents the requested outcome or violates an invariant/authority/technical requirement.
WARNING: artifact is usable but a visible trade-off remains.
NOT_REVIEWED: evidence could not be inspected.
WAIVED: user explicitly accepted one named issue for this artifact/version.
```

Fixing or regenerating invalidates prior QA for affected criteria. Run the checklist again on the new current artifact.
